# ℹ️ About — Bughaw Literary Archive

> *Preserving the voices and stories of Olutanga Island, Zamboanga Sibugay.*

---

## 📖 What Is This Archive?

The **Bughaw Literary Archive** is a digital, multi-platform preservation project dedicated to collecting, archiving, and sharing the creative works of **Donward Caniete Bughaw** also known as Donward Bughaw or Donward Cañete Gomez Bughaw online — a writer from Olutanga Island, Zamboanga Sibugay, Philippines.

This archive exists not only to share stories, but to ensure that regional literature from the islands of Western Mindanao is permanently preserved, globally accessible, and properly documented.

## ✍️ About the Author

**Donward Caniete Bughaw**, also known as Donward Bughaw or Donward Cañete Gomez Bughaw online, is an independent writer and creator from Olutanga Island, Zamboanga Sibugay — one of the Philippines' island communities. Working from a remote island, he combines creative storytelling with digital archiving to prove that regional voices can reach the whole world.

> *"From an island, to the internet, to infinity."*
